The challenge

Northbeam was scaling its dispatch team from 8 to 25 people in six months. Each new hire took roughly two weeks to become productive on the routing system, mostly through shadowing a senior dispatcher — a process that broke down once two hires started in the same week and there weren’t enough seniors to go around.

Why they chose Ramp Ops

Northbeam’s ops director had tried a generic LMS tool first, but it didn’t reflect their actual routing logic. Ramp Ops stood out because the onboarding flow could be built directly from Northbeam’s own dispatch recordings rather than generic training content.

What we did

  1. Recorded 12 real dispatch sessions with senior staff narrating decisions
  2. Converted recordings into a 9-module interactive walkthrough
  3. Built a scenario-based quiz bank from real edge cases (rerouting, weather delays)
  4. Set up a Slack-based check-in flow for new hires’ first two weeks
